Last updated: May 2026

Your data is used solely to: identify you in the system, process booking requests, and enable in-app messaging between clients and barbers.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with service providers necessary to operate the app (e.g., hosting, authentication).

4. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
